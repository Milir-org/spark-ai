Redesign the creation entry flow of SPARK so the product structure is clear before we redesign the rest of the app.

Context:
The current product is confusing because PPC is carrying too much of the whole platform.
SPARK actually supports two real-world modes:
1. integrated multi-channel campaigns
2. standalone channel campaigns such as PPC-only

We need to fix the entry architecture first.
Do not redesign the whole app yet.
Do not do a broad visual overhaul yet.
Focus only on the first creation decision and the product structure around it.

Goal:
When a user wants to create work in SPARK, the app should clearly ask what kind of campaign they want to create.

New creation model:
The create flow must start with two choices:

1. Integrated Campaign
Use for:
- product launches
- cross-channel demand generation
- regional campaigns
- campaigns involving multiple channels and shared budget

Integrated Campaign characteristics:
- has one Campaign Owner
- can assign multiple Channel Owners
- has shared objective, audience, budget, approvals, and deliverables
- channels receive work from the master campaign blueprint

2. Channel Campaign
Use for:
- BAU work
- standalone PPC / SEO / CRM / Social campaigns
- channel-specific execution not requiring full cross-channel orchestration

Channel Campaign characteristics:
- has one primary owner, usually the Channel Owner
- may optionally link to a parent integrated campaign
- can operate independently

Important product rule:
Do not force users into PPC first.
Do not assume every campaign starts as a PPC workflow.

Required UX change:
Add a proper first creation screen that asks:
“What do you want to create?”

This screen should present two clear options:
- Integrated Campaign
- Channel Campaign

If user chooses Channel Campaign:
show channel choices such as:
- PPC
- SEO
- Social
- CRM
- Messaging
- Creative

If user chooses Integrated Campaign:
route into a high-level campaign brief flow
Do not build the entire brief flow yet unless needed.
A lightweight placeholder or partial flow is acceptable.
The important thing is to establish the correct product structure.

Ownership model to reflect in the UI:
- Campaign Owner
- Channel Owner
- Contributor
- Approver

For now, show this structurally in the create flow where relevant.
Do not overbuild permissions logic yet.

Information architecture intent:
SPARK should clearly separate:
- integrated campaign orchestration
- channel-level execution

Deliverables for this task:
1. redesign the creation entry flow
2. make the two campaign modes clear in the UI
3. stop using PPC as the default implied creation model
4. keep the app runnable
5. keep the implementation coherent and focused

Important constraints:
- do not redesign the entire PPC workflow in this task
- do not attempt a full visual overhaul in this task
- do not overcomplicate backend changes unless necessary
- focus on getting the product architecture and first user decision right

Critical instruction:
This task is only about fixing the first creation decision and clarifying product structure.
Do not turn this into another broad refactor.
